---

### Project Scope

**Type:** 4-page microsite (Homepage, About, Resources/Newsroom, Contact)
**Purpose:** Generate interest and drive conversation requests while protecting proprietary details
**Target Audiences:** Investors, Research Hospitals, Vendors

---

### Risk Register

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Logo/brand not finalized | Blocks all design | Prioritize logo selection in Week 1 |
| Investor content sensitivity | Legal review needed | Flag "NEED TO DISCUSS" items early |
| Salesforce integration complexity | Delays form routing | Use simple email routing as fallback |
| Hero imagery not provided | Blocks homepage design | Use abstract/nature imagery per "Truths" deck |
