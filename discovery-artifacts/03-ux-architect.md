### User Personas & Journeys

#### Persona 1: Institutional Investor
**Goal:** Evaluate Person Health as investment opportunity
**Journey:**
1. Lands on Homepage (likely from referral or direct link)
2. Reads Hero + Platform overview
3. Scrolls to "For Investors" section
4. Clicks CTA: "Contact us for investor materials"
5. Fills segmented contact form ("I'm an Investor")
6. Receives follow-up from Person Health team

**Key Content Needs:** Market opportunity, competitive differentiation, team credibility, clinical validation partners

#### Persona 2: Research Hospital Administrator / Physician
**Goal:** Explore clinical partnership opportunity
**Journey:**
1. Lands on Homepage
2. Reads Key Advantages (clinical validation, CLIA/CAP)
3. Scrolls to "For Research Hospitals" section
4. Reviews partnership opportunities
5. Clicks CTA: "Request Clinical Partnership Discussion"
6. Fills segmented contact form ("I'm a Health System")

**Key Content Needs:** Clinical validation evidence, partnership model, co-publication opportunities, pilot program details

#### Persona 3: Vendor / Technology Partner
**Goal:** Explore integration or supply partnership
**Journey:**
1. Lands on Homepage
2. Reads Platform section to understand technology
3. Scrolls to "For Vendors" section
4. Reviews integration opportunities
5. Clicks CTA: "Submit Vendor Inquiry"
6. Fills segmented contact form ("I'm a Vendor / Partner")

**Key Content Needs:** Technical capabilities, scale/growth trajectory, IP protection, co-development model

### Content Hierarchy (Homepage)

1. **Hero** (emotional hook + positioning)
2. **Key Advantages** (credibility at a glance)
3. **Platform Overview** (what we do)
4. **Modalities Diagram** (how it works)
5. **Audience Sections** (why it matters to YOU)
6. **CTA Bar** (what to do next)

This follows the classic: Hook → Credibility → Explanation → Relevance → Action

---

### Accessibility Requirements

- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
- Keyboard navigation for all interactive elements
- Screen reader labels on icons and diagrams
- Color contrast ratio 4.5:1 minimum
- Focus indicators on all form fields and buttons
- Alt text on all images
- Semantic HTML (header, nav, main, section, footer)
