# Agent 04: Brand Guardian
## Person Health - Brand Guidelines & Design Foundation

---

### Brand Positioning

**Tagline:** "Detect Disease Before Symptoms. Understand Health for Life."
**Positioning Statement:** The first multi-modal, AI-enabled platform detecting disease before symptoms appear.
**Brand Promise:** Three companies, one integrated Health OS.

---

### Brand Personality

| Attribute | Expression |
|-----------|-----------|
| Credible | Data-backed claims, clinical partner logos, patent counts |
| Serious | No casual language, no consumer health playfulness |
| Visionary | Forward-looking language about health infrastructure |
| Human | "The Person, not the provider, is the Platform" |
| Precise | Specific without revealing proprietary details |

**Tone:** Serious, credible, investor-oriented. Similar to Boston Scientific, Medtronic, Abbott in clarity and professionalism.

---

### Logo Options Under Review

Six directions were presented. Key candidates and their brand fit:

| Logo | Style | Colors | Brand Fit |
|------|-------|--------|-----------|
| **A** | Script "Person" + Bold "Health" | Teal #024653 / Green #05D16E | Warm + professional; human connection |
| **B** | Modern geometric with "spark" accents | Blue #195BFE / Light #E5ECFE | Clinical, innovative, medical-grade |
| **C** | Bold caps with human figure in "T" | Purple #6237A0 / Dark #28104E | Introspective, credible, stable |

**Recommendation for investor-oriented medtech site:** Logo B or C align most closely with the stated tone (Boston Scientific, Medtronic comparables). Logo A leans more consumer/wellness.

### Typography Recommendations

| Use | Font | Weight | Rationale |
|-----|------|--------|-----------|
| Headlines | Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans | 700 (Bold) | Clean, modern, medtech-appropriate |
| Body | Inter | 400 (Regular) | Excellent readability, open source |
| Accent/Data | Space Grotesk or IBM Plex Mono | 500 | Technical credibility for stats/metrics |

---

### Photography & Imagery Direction

From the "Truths" deck, the visual language uses:
- **Nature photography** (oceans, skies, forests, snow) - representing possibility and clarity
- **Patient photography** (real people, not stock-feeling) - representing humanity
- **Blue gradient overlays** on images - creating visual consistency

**Guidelines:**
- Use abstract/nature imagery for hero sections
- Use real photography (not illustrations) for trust
- Apply brand-color gradient overlays (blue or purple per logo choice)
- Avoid: generic stock medical imagery (stethoscopes, lab coats)

---

### Voice & Messaging Guardrails

**DO say:**
- "Infrastructure for early understanding"
- "Multi-modal Health OS"
- "Clinically validated"
- "Detect disease before symptoms"
- "Lifelong health stewardship"

**DO NOT say:**
- Specific revenue figures or unit economics on public pages
- Trial endpoints or unpublished metrics
- "Cure" or "treatment" (this is detection, not therapy)
- Specific patient counts beyond "100K+ patients ready"
- Unsubstantiated superlatives ("best," "only," "guaranteed")
