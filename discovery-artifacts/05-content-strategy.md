### Content Principles

1. **Lead with the problem, not the product** - Mirror the "Truths" narrative (disease is a surprise, data is fragmented, patients are powerless)
2. **Protect proprietary details** - High-level language; no specifics on revenue, trial data, or unpublished metrics
3. **Signal credibility through association** - Mayo Clinic, Cleveland Clinic, MD Anderson, Baylor, Boston Scientific lineage
4. **Create urgency without hype** - "9.4M missed cancer screenings post-COVID" (from investor deck)
5. **Audience-specific relevance** - Each visitor type should find their section within 10 seconds
