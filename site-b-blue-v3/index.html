Our Story

Foundational Belief: Disease should be detected before symptoms appear. Every person deserves access to the kind of early detection that saves lives.

Mission: To build the world's first integrated multi-modal diagnostic platform that makes early disease detection accessible, accurate, and affordable.

Origins: Person Health was built from the convergence of a University of Minnesota spinout (VocxiHealth), molecular diagnostics innovator Oncodea, and the laboratory infrastructure of MTL/CareNexa. Together, these organizations form the foundation of the Health OS.

Team: Our scientific founders hold 80+ patents across chemistry, sensors, and machine learning. Our leadership team brings decades of experience from Boston Scientific, Mayo Clinic, and leading health technology companies.

Building a Community of Change

We believe that the future of healthcare is collaborative. Person Health connects patients, clinicians, researchers, and technologists around a shared mission: detecting disease earlier.
